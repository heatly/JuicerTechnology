# Strategy (current scope)

**Positioning:** Real-world data for physical AI. Juicer sources consented, first-person (egocentric) video of skilled work to train robots and world models, and builds robotics that learn from it.

**Sitemap**
```

/egocentric-data    What it is, data types, scarcity, environments, pipeline, offers, principles, contributor form
/robotics           Tracks, loop, capabilities, pilot form
```

**Flows**
- Buyer: Home or Egocentric Data, Request data form, email or Sheet, scoping call, sample or collection brief.
- Robotics partner: Robotics, Pilot form, scoping call.
- Contributor: Egocentric Data, contributor form, local hub contact, separate consent, recording.

**Design:** dark-first, cyan/lime accent for data (volt), orange for robotics and the logo. Animated neural mesh, point-cloud scene, rig diagram, animated robot arm, flowing pipeline, log-scale gap bars. All honour reduced motion.

**Source material:** ego.mom (thesis, contributor model), plus the public positioning of Objectways, Build AI and Abundance, rewritten in original words with approximate figures.
