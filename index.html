FAQ

Common Questions

How far in advance should I book my event?
+

We recommend reaching out at least 4–6 weeks in advance for medium events (100–500 people) and 8–12 weeks for large conferences. For smaller meetups, we can often accommodate 2–3 weeks notice.

Why does pricing vary by city?
+

Venue costs, catering rates, transport logistics, and speaker travel expenses differ significantly between cities. Bangalore and Mumbai typically have higher base costs than Tier-2 cities. We provide a transparent, itemized quote for every city.

Can I customize the swags with my brand?
+

Absolutely! All swag items are fully customizable with your brand logo, colors, taglines, and even custom packaging. We handle design, printing, and delivery to your event venue.

Do you manage virtual or hybrid events?
+

Yes! We support fully virtual, fully in-person, and hybrid formats. For hybrid events we coordinate AV setups, streaming platforms, and virtual attendee engagement alongside the physical event.

What is the minimum order for swags?
+

Minimum quantities vary by item — typically 25 units for premium items like hoodies and 50 units for t-shirts and bottles. Stickers and notebooks have lower minimums. Our team will advise the best options for your attendee count.

How do I get a quote?
+

Simply fill out the event request form above! Select your swags, fill in the event details, and submit. Our team will reach out via email within 24 hours with a customized quote based on your city and requirements.
